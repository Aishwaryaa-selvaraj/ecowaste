About ECOWASTE

Households identify and segregate recyclable waste, see what it is worth, and book a pickup. Collectors manage pickups, weigh each material transparently and deliver the waste to recyclers. Recyclers record the recycling progress. Everyone gets a clear record of every kilogram and every rupee.

Environmental Awareness

Segregate at source

Keeping wet and dry waste apart makes recycling and composting far easier and keeps recyclables clean and valuable.

Metals save energy

Recycling aluminium uses roughly 95% less energy than making it from raw ore.

Compost food waste

Kitchen waste can become compost instead of methane-producing landfill.
